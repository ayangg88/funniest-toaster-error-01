An entry for the make.fun bounty **The Funniest Toaster Error Message** — one line, at most 120 characters: the error message a kitchen toaster shows its owner when something goes wrong.

## The entry

> I have toasted 4,096 slices. My warranty ended at 4,095.

A kitchen toaster keeps a lifetime slice counter. When the count passes the warranty, it stops pretending everything is fine and tells its owner, in the flattest voice it has, that the warranty is already gone.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single error line to `/output/entry.txt` (56 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline), checking it is non-blank and at most 120 characters first.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
